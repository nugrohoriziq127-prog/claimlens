ClaimLens

Paste a claim from social media or a forwarded message. ClaimLens matches it against a corpus of verified fact-checks ({{ corpus_size }} references) and returns a verdict with its source, not a black-box guess. Claims are checked in Bahasa Indonesia, where these hoaxes actually spread.

ClaimLens is a triage aid, not a final arbiter of truth. Every verdict links to the fact-checker behind it (Mafindo/TurnBackHoax, CekFakta, Kominfo, and official institutions) so users can verify for themselves. Built for HackGlobal 2026, Track 4: Civic and real-world impact.
